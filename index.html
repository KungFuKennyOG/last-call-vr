Last Call

Miami '89

Sunday, 6 a.m., a concrete power-station hall on two floors, with no phones and no mercy. Hold it with two pistols against everyone coming through the doors and down the stairs. Everything you drop goes down as a ragdoll.

Map

Checking for a headset…

Showdowns: six short story levels with a small hop and bullet time. Noir New York, a Hong Kong teahouse, a war-torn village, 1929 Chicago, a favela, Tokyo in the rain.

Trigger
Fire. One pistol per hand.
Move
Left stick runs, click it to walk. Y (or click the right stick) is bullet time. A jumps (again in mid-air), B or X dashes. Right stick snap-turns. Kills in the air or mid-dash score ×1.5.
Launch pads
Step on a red pad. The two on the floor throw you onto the balcony; the one upstairs throws you back across the hall.
Pillars
Stop bullets both ways. Use them.
Stairs
Two runs along the side walls lead up to the balcony. They use them too, and they shoot down from up there.
Melee
Swing a gun hard to pistol-whip whatever it hits. In the browser, press F or right-click.
Downed
Gut and leg shots can drop them alive. They crawl, groan and bleed out, unless you finish them.
Reload
Point a gun at the floor. It spins and reloads.
Red tracers
Dodge for slow-mo, or shoot them out of the air.
Guns
Uzis and sawed-offs turn up on the floor and drop from gunners and heavies. Grab with the grip button.
Frenzy
Every kill puts two rounds back. Five in a row: five seconds of double fire rate and no reloads.
Listen
Doors slam where they open. They flank you, throw bottles and dodge your laser.
Wrist
Wave, score and streak live on your left gun.

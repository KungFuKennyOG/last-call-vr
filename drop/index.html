Last Drop

Two couriers, one rainy night, and a sealed bag every crew in the city wants. Seven chapters from 11 p.m. to sunrise. This build has chapter 1.

01Wrong Order11 p.m. · a ghost kitchen · solo

Checking for a headset…

Trigger
Fire. A pistol in each hand. Point a gun at the floor to reload.
Move
Left stick walks, right stick snap-turns, A or X hops. Doors open when you walk into them.
The bag
Grab it off the counter. It rides on your back. Grabbers go for it, so drop them before they reach a door.
Bullet time
Y or click the right stick. Kills refill it.
Stunned
The world goes grey. Grip near your chest for the adrenaline shot, then stab it down into your thigh.
Grip
Pick things up: an Uzi, a cleaver, the extinguisher. With nothing in reach, grip throws what you picked up; B throws anything, even your pistol. A thrown gun stuns, a thrown cleaver kills.
Codes
At the end of the chapter you get a password code. Type it here (or on the keypad in VR) to keep your rating. Cheats go in the same box.
